## 6. Video Shot List

### Intro
- Black screen, then the deck at dawn — steam rising, crew in yellow vests
- Slow push-in on the Super Hornet on the catapult, rhino horn catching first light

### Verse 1
- Catapult shot — the plane accelerating, steam rolling off the deck
- Pilot's face in the cockpit, visor down, hands on the stick
- Wide shot of the carrier cutting through the ocean

### Chorus
- Rhino horn in close-up as the plane banks, sun flaring off the antenna
- Formation of Hornets banking together, contrails in blue sky
- Deck crew watching, saluting, the plane disappearing into the horizon

### Verse 2
- Interior cockpit — instruments, the pilot's breath, the horizon tilting
- Afterburner glow, the plane climbing hard
- Clouds parting, the sky opening up

### Chorus
- Rhino horn catching the light again — tighter, faster cuts
- The plane pulling hard, G-force on the pilot's face
- Wide shot of the ocean far below, the carrier shrinking

### Bridge
- Slow-motion of the plane banking, the horn glinting
- The pilot's hand on the throttle, knuckles white
- A single tear of oil or sweat on the canopy

### Verse 3
- The plane diving, the deck rushing up
- Arresting hook catching the wire — the jolt, the stop
- Crew rushing in, the plane settling on the deck

### Outro
- The plane parked, engines winding down, the horn still warm
- The pilot climbing out, helmet under arm, looking back at the sky
- Fade to black on the rhino horn in silhouette against the sunset

- Match cuts to the breath, not just the beat
- When the lyric says "the sky opens up," cut wide
