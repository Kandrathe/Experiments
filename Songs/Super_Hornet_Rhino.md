## 2. Summary

A first-person carrier pilot anthem. The Rhino (F/A-18 Super Hornet) launches from the deck, climbs to Angels 30, paints targets, drops JDAM, then traps the wire. The hook is simple and physical: get your flames on, light 'em up, fight tonight. The lyric is packed with real deck language — catapult kick, call the ball, catch the wire, Betty's bitchin', folded wings, AIM-9 / AIM-120 — so the video can cut on the words, not around them.

## 6. Video Shot List (cut on the words)

### Intro — Get your flames on / Feel the heat
- Afterburner cans lighting, night or dusk
- Heat shimmer off the deck
- Tight on the flame, then pull back to the whole jet

### Verse 1 — Racing hearts… Catapult kick
- Deck crew running (yellow shirts, moving feet)
- Afterburner glow as the sky splits
- Twin tails from behind as it banks into cold air
- Wide: carrier deck to open ocean
- **Catapult kick** — steam shot, the plane slams forward. Cut on the word *kick*

### Pre-Chorus — Visor down / Rhino rolls
- Pilot visor coming down, one breath in the mask
- Rhino horn (IFF antenna) rolling into launch position
- Hands on the stick, claim-the-crown energy

### Chorus — Get your flames on
- Afterburners full, night sparks / deck lights
- Fast cuts: flame, sky, deck, cockpit HUD
- Hold wide on *fight tonight*

### Verse 2 — That's my jet… call the ball
- Gray jet on the scene, close beauty pass
- Intake / scream — love her bite
- LSO platform: *call the ball* — meatball, Clara if you have a face for her
- Trap approach lining up (*gonna trap my move*)

### Verse 3 — GE engines… catch the wire
- Engine close-up, vapor cone at the rim (transonic)
- AIM-9 / AIM-120 on the rails (whispers / sings)
- Wings folding on deck (*folded wings*)
- Climbing — altimeter / HUD to Angels 30
- **Catch the wire** — hook down, slam, stop. Cut on *wire*
- Caution panel / Bitchin' Betty audio if you can license or recreate it

### Bridge — Radar / JDAM
- Cockpit radar, ghostly green paint
- Lock-on tone (audio first, then HUD lock box)
- JDAM leaving the rail, falling through blue
- Return to visor / breath / *Rhino ready*

### Guitar Solo
- Beauty reel: bank, climb, deck receding, ocean glare
- Let the jet fly. Don't cut every bar.

### Verse 4 — That's my Rhino
- Repeat verse-2 beauty shots, faster
- Mach 1.8 — vapor, shock diamond if you have it
- Sea and sky rattling — low pass over water, then over the boat
- Last goodbye — jet disappearing into haze

### Final Chorus / Outro
- Stack the flame shots. Three *get your flames on* = three burner hits
- Outro: cans dying down, heat still in the air, fade on the horn silhouette

## Notes

- Cut on the breath and on the nouns: *kick*, *visor*, *ball*, *wire*, *flames*.
- Public-domain / official Navy demo footage exists for catapults, traps, vapor cones, folding wings. Match the lyric, don't invent hardware that isn't on an F/A-18E/F.
- Clara = LSO / ball call. If she stays in the lyric, give her one face shot on *Can't you see, Clara?*
