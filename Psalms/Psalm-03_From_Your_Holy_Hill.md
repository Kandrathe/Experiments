# Psalm 3 — From Your Holy Hill I'm Led

## 1. Original Text (KJV)

1 Lord, how are they increased that trouble me! many are they that rise up against me.

2 Many there be which say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. Selah.

3 But thou, O Lord, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.

4 I cried unto the Lord with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.

5 I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me.

6 I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set themselves against me round about.

7 Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.

8 Salvation belongeth unto the Lord: thy blessing is upon thy people. Selah.

---

## 2. Summary
David tells God how many enemies have risen up against him.  His foes mock his faith, saying God will not help him.Meaning: David is honest about his pain and the size of his problem. He brings his raw fear directly to God in prayer. David calls God a "shield around me," his "glory," and the one who "lifts my head high".  He cries out, and God answers from His holy hill.Meaning: David shifts his focus from his enemies to God. The word "shield" means total protection on every side—above, beneath, and within. God restores his honor and confidence (lifting his head).  Prayer for Deliverance and Praise David asks God to rise up, save him, and strike his enemies on the cheek or break their teeth.  He ends by declaring that salvation belongs to the Lord and asks for God's blessing on His people.  Meaning: David leaves justice and victory in God's hands rather than trying to fix everything by his own power.
