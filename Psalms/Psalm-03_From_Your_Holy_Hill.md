
[Verse 1]
Lord, how they rise to trouble me,
Many stand against my soul.
They whisper low, “No help from God,”
Yet my heart won’t bear their toll.
Selah (hum or pause)

[Chorus]
But Thou, O Lord, my shield, my glory,
Lifter of my weary head.
I cry aloud, You hear my voice,
From Your holy hill, I’m led.

[Verse 2]
I lay me down, I sleep, I wake,
For the Lord sustains my frame.
Ten thousand foes surround my path,
But I’ll never fear their flame.
Selah (hum or pause)

[Chorus]
But Thou, O Lord, my shield, my glory,
Lifter of my weary head.
I cry aloud, You hear my voice,
From Your holy hill, I’m led.

[Verse 3]
Arise, O Lord, and save me now,
Smite my foes, their teeth You break.
Salvation’s Yours, Your blessing falls,
On Your people for Your sake.
Selah (hum or pause)
